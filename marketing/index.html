03 — Voice

Capture a plan or activity without opening the whole app.

Voice is an input method for LIFE OS data, not a separate assistant. Say what you need, review the parsed result, then it lands in plans or actual time.

  • NaturalSpeak ordinary phrases instead of rigid commands.
  • ParsedTime, duration and context become structured fields.
  • DesktopA global desktop voice flow is part of LIFE OS.
  • ReviewThe parsed result stays visible before committing changes.

04 — Plan vs fact

The useful question is not “was I productive?”

What did I intend to do, what actually happened, and what pattern should change next time? LIFE OS keeps both sides of the day in the same model.
